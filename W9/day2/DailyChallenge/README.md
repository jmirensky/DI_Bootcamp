# US Superstore Data Analysis

This notebook analyzes the US Superstore dataset to explore sales trends, product performance, and the relationship between discount and profit.

---

## Key Insights

1. **Sales Trend Over Years:**  
 - After a dip in 2015, sales grew consistently through 2017.  
- The business ended 2017 much larger than it started, indicating strong recovery and growth potential.

2. **Sales Distribution by Country:**  
   Dataset contains only the United States; the total sales are concentrated there.

3. **Top 10 Products by Sales:**  
   - Top 10 products contribute ~10.65% of total sales.  
   - 4 out of 10 top-selling products have zero or negative profit, showing that high sales do not guarantee profitability.

4. **Profit vs Discount:**  
   There is a negative correlation between discount and profit. Higher discounts tend to reduce overall profit.
